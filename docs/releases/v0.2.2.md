PinkCollab v0.2.2 — easier session switching and safer history recovery

## Highlights

- The Sessions screen has a scrollable strip of compact cards. Tap a card to jump to its timeline; swiping the timeline follows the selected card without making strip scrolling change sessions.
- Cards show the session title, workspace folder, and status. The website now includes a PinkCollab illustration.

## Fixes

- A new session whose OMP transcript has not yet been written can show empty history and resume after its runtime stops.
- Once a prompt may have written history, an absent transcript is reported as unavailable instead of silently treating the session as empty or replacing its OMP mapping, including after an aborted prompt.

## Upgrade

Install the 0.2.2 app and Gateway together. The Gateway upgrades its database schema from v2 to v3 on startup, preserving existing pairings and protecting older mapped sessions. Stop the previous Gateway before starting 0.2.2.
